# Golden Era v0.1.323 (Preview)

> **This is a preview build, not a finished release.** It still has issues. Most units have had an animation pass against the original HoMM3 animations, but some are still being polished, and some problems will only show up in play.

> **Want the newest fixes?** The [nightly build](https://github.com/leviritchie/Golden-Era/releases/tag/nightly) is rebuilt automatically as fixes land. It has more fixes than this release but is less validated: nightlies are not hand-tested. If a bug blocks you here, try the nightly.

## What changed since v0.1.322

### New faction abilities

These are new this release and have **not yet been tested in game**. Please report anything that looks off.

- **Cove, Treasure Hunting Plunder:** treasure chests add a small amount of a random rare resource, scaled by rank. A hero's first capture of each mine adds a resource pile of its type.
- **Tower, Chronomancy, Borrowed Time:** a new hero action. It costs 2 Focus, has a 3-round cooldown, and gives a friendly stack +100 Initiative for one round.
- **Dungeon, Captives:** Captives replaces Draconic Dominion. Fleeing enemy squads that a human hero lets go become captives, which give +1 to +5 Attack and Defense tiers. Captives are spent by Execute (for XP) or Ransom (for gold). A shared hero-skill meter sits beside the hero portrait.
- **Fortress, Hunting Grounds:** Hunting Grounds replaces Swamp Tactics. Battle wins fill a per-hero Trophy meter. A creeping enemy applies an Initiative and Speed penalty. A full meter restocks this week's visited weekly sites once. The meter empties each week.

### Town buildings

- **Tower Mage Guild** has its fifth level.
- **Tower training building** is now the HoMM3 Wall of Knowledge. Any visiting hero gets +1 Knowledge, with no click needed.
- Town buildings without authored art are skipped, rather than showing a black block in the town view.

### Neutral dwellings

- Neutral dwellings at tier 4 and above have their guards set correctly.

### Battle and terrain

- **Alt Pristine Unicorn** is smaller, and **alt Diamond Dragon** is larger in battle, so they read correctly next to other units.
- Large multi-terrain maps stay under the terrain shader's 16-slot limit, so they no longer fail to render.

## Known issues

- The new faction abilities above are not yet tested in game.
- Known issues from v0.1.322 still apply unless noted here: the vampire line turns into mist while moving without a travelling effect, and a few neutral units are still in their animation pass.

Nightly builds are untested prereleases. If the problem persists on that build, please reply on the issue tracker.
