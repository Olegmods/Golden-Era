# Golden Era v0.1.324 (Preview)

> **This is a preview build, not a finished release.** It still has issues. Most units have had an animation pass against the original HoMM3 animations, but some are still being polished, and some problems will only show up in play.

> **Want the newest fixes?** The [nightly build](https://github.com/leviritchie/Golden-Era/releases/tag/nightly) is rebuilt automatically as fixes land. It has more fixes than this release but is less validated: nightlies are not hand-tested. If a bug blocks you here, try the nightly.

## What changed since v0.1.323

### Town scenes

The 3D town scenes have been rebuilt for every faction except Conflux.

- **Sharper towns.** Town art is upscaled 4x, so buildings and backdrops stay crisp when you zoom and pan.
- **Real depth.** Each town is rebuilt as one continuous 3D scene, without separate panels. Ground, buildings and backdrop sit at believable distances. Streaks and smears behind buildings while you orbit the camera are greatly reduced.
- **Living towns.** Every town has its own ambient effects: about 45 to 65 per town, with effects unique to each faction. Examples: Inferno's ash, lava bubbles and heat haze; Bulwark's snowfall, ravens and a real rippling aurora; Necropolis' own mists and gloom.
- **Background houses are real objects.** In Factory, Castle, Stronghold and Bulwark, the houses and huts painted into the backdrop are now their own 3D pieces on clean ground. Castle's distant cottages sit on the ground instead of floating.
- **Cove** has been rebuilt several times over. The tavern, halls and other buildings sit on their painted ground, and the cliffs and falls sit behind the town.
- Black outlines no longer appear around layered town pieces.

### Faction abilities

The four faction abilities added in v0.1.323 did nothing in game. They are now wired up:

- **Cove, Plunder:** reads the hero's skill rank, rewards opening treasure chests, and rewards the first capture of each mine.
- **Tower, Borrowed Time:** the hero action now comes with the skill as described in its text.
- **Dungeon, Captives:** letting a fleeing enemy go now creates captives.
- **Fortress, Hunting Grounds:** the Trophy meter now fills and its effects apply.
- **Tower, Mana Well aegis:** the check now detects the skill correctly.

### Units

- **Azure Dragon** and **Rust Dragon** fly above the floor when moving, with the chest level and the neck stretched forward, as in HoMM3.
- **Sharpshooter** turns side-on and draws the longbow when shooting, as in HoMM3.

## Known issues

- The faction abilities above have not yet been re-tested in game. Please report anything that looks off.
- The Conflux town scene has not been rebuilt yet and still uses the older version.
- Town scenes can still show small streaks at some camera angles.
- Known issues from earlier releases still apply unless noted here. For example, the vampire line turns into mist while moving without a travelling effect, and a few neutral units are still in their animation pass.

Nightly builds are untested prereleases. If the problem persists on that build, please reply on the issue tracker.
