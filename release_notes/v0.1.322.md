# Golden Era v0.1.322 (Preview)

> **This is a preview build, not a finished release.** It still has issues. Every faction's units have now had an animation pass against the original HoMM3 animations, but a handful of units are still being polished, and some problems will only show up in play.

> **Want the newest fixes?** The [nightly build](https://github.com/leviritchie/Golden-Era/releases/tag/nightly) is rebuilt automatically as fixes land. It has more fixes than this release but is less validated: nightlies are not hand-tested. If a bug blocks you here, try the nightly.

## What changed since v0.1.321

### Unit animations: HoMM3 motion pass for every faction

v0.1.321 shipped the Castle pass and the start of Rampart. This release covers every remaining faction: Rampart, Tower, Inferno, Necropolis, Dungeon, Stronghold, Fortress, Conflux, Cove, Factory, Bulwark and the neutral creatures.

Each unit was compared clip by clip with its original HoMM3 animation: idle, move, attack, ranged, defend, hit and death. The aim is the same intent and readability as HoMM3, with natural 3D motion rather than a stiff frame-by-frame copy. Highlights:

- **Attacks aim at the target.** Thrusts, swings, shots and breath attacks now read clearly at battle distance, with the head and body turned toward the enemy.
- **Flyers:** wing beats and rearing forelimbs move properly instead of a stiff, posed look. Griffins keep their folded-wing idle on the ground and spread their wings in flight.
- **Fixed broken pieces:** many mesh-surgery fixes remove floating fragments, weapons welded to limbs and joints that tore while moving.
- **Stride, not slow motion:** move speeds match comparable Olden Era units, and walks use longer strides.
- **Factory:**
  - The Automaton line extends its telescoping arm on the attack.
  - The Juggernaut punches straight at its target.
  - The Gunslinger's alternate upgrade levels its rifle and sights down the barrel when shooting, and makes a level bayonet lunge in melee.
- **Neutrals:** the Crystal Dragon sits back on its haunches like the original.

### Unit visual effects in the Olden Era style

The old effects ripped from HoMM3 sprite animations are gone. They have been replaced with effects built in the style of Olden Era's own units: emissive glows, casts, breath, sparks and auras that fire on the right frames of each animation. They also show in unit previews, not only in battle.

Over 100 units have them, including:
- the Elementals;
- the Angel, Archangel and Seraph;
- the Monk, Priest and Zealot;
- the Unicorn line;
- the Gold, Green, Diamond, Azure, Rust, Faerie, Ghost and Spectral Dragons;
- the Genie line;
- the Phoenix and Sacred Phoenix;
- the vampires and liches;
- the Cove casters;
- Factory steam and flame.

The **Sacred Phoenix** is now a recolor of the Firebird with pale-gold phoenix fire.

### Look

- **Vanilla-style finish:** unit textures follow Olden Era's own shading philosophy, so they sit better next to the base game's units.
- **Metal shine:** armour and weapons on metal-clad units catch the light with a dynamic highlight.

### Fixes

- The installer now bundles BepInEx's Unity base library, so the first launch needs no extra download.

## Known issues

- The vampire line turns into mist while moving, but the travelling bat or mist effect isn't in yet, so the unit vanishes during the move.
- A few neutral units are still in their animation pass.
